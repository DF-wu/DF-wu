# 06 Atlas

Atlas is the evidence-map version of the portfolio.

It treats ChuFei Wu as a systems engineer whose identity is best understood through routes:

- research route: CCTS, event-driven contracts, APSEC 2022, TCSE award
- delivery route: PDAS, fintech/payment, CI/CD, AKS, customer-facing delivery
- operations route: home-lab services, iDRAC automation, internal systems maintenance
- collaboration route: OSS integrations, localization, documentation correctness

Design direction:

- editorial atlas layout with visible grid structure and hard evidence bands
- light paper surface, ink text, cobalt/pine/brass/coral accents
- avatar as the main visual asset, used as identity evidence rather than decoration
- sharp 8px cards, dense but readable information hierarchy
- branch default style: `atlas`
